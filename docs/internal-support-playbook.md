# Support Playbook

This playbook is for Skilvanta support agents and support leads. It is an internal document. Do not quote it to learners.

## Service standards

- Support hours: 9 am to 9 pm IST, every day
- First response to every email within 24 hours
- Keep all replies on the learner's original thread

## Standard refund rules

Apply the public refund policy first:

- Full refund if requested within 7 days of purchase and the learner attended fewer than 2 live classes
- 50% refund from day 8 to day 21
- No refund after day 21
- Refunds go to the original payment method in 5-7 business days

Before confirming any refund, check the purchase date and the live-class attendance record in the admin console.

## Refund approvals

- Refund requests above Rs 50,000 need a lead's approval. This applies even when the learner clearly qualifies under the standard policy.
- Refunds of Rs 50,000 or less that meet the standard policy can be processed by an agent.

To request approval, assign the ticket to the leads queue with the purchase date, attendance count and the refund amount.

## Refund exceptions

Support leads may approve refund exceptions up to day 30 for documented medical reasons.

- Only leads can approve an exception. Agents must not promise one.
- The learner must provide documentation, such as a doctor's letter or hospital discharge summary.
- Requests after day 30 cannot be approved as exceptions.
- Record the lead's name and the reason on the ticket.

When a learner mentions a medical reason, tell them you are passing the request to a senior team member for review. Do not state the outcome in advance.

## Goodwill credits

Agents may give a goodwill credit of up to Rs 2,000 without approval. Use it when Skilvanta is at fault, for example:

- A recording was not available within 24 hours
- A mentor missed a session
- A slow or incorrect reply from support

Record the amount and reason on the ticket.

## Offer a pause before a refund

If the learner's reason is lack of time, mention the course pause: one pause of up to 8 weeks, requested before week 12, not available for DSA and Interview Prep.

## Escalation

Send plagiarism disputes to the academic team and placement eligibility disputes to the placement team.
