# DSA and Interview Prep

## At a glance

- Duration: 10 weeks
- Fee: Rs 30,000
- Format: self-paced, with one live doubt session every Sunday at 6 pm IST
- Prerequisites: none

## Who it is for

This course is for people preparing for coding interviews. It suits:

- Final-year students preparing for campus and off-campus hiring
- Working developers planning a job switch
- Learners finishing another Skilvanta course who want focused interview practice

There are no prerequisites. The first week starts from complexity analysis and arrays. You can solve problems in any mainstream language.

## Course outline

- Week 1: Complexity Analysis, Arrays and Strings
- Week 2: Hashing, Two Pointers and Sliding Window
- Week 3: Linked Lists, Stacks and Queues
- Week 4: Recursion and Backtracking
- Week 5: Trees and Binary Search Trees
- Week 6: Heaps and Tries
- Week 7: Graphs
- Week 8: Dynamic Programming
- Week 9: Greedy Algorithms, Binary Search and Bit Manipulation
- Week 10: Mock Interviews and Revision

## How the course runs

Each week unlocks a set of video lessons and a graded problem set. You work through them at your own pace during the week.

Every Sunday at 6 pm IST there is a live doubt session where an instructor works through the problems learners found hardest. Bring your questions. The session is recorded and available within 24 hours.

You also get two 1:1 mentor sessions per month, which many learners use for mock interview practice.

## Things that differ from other courses

- No-cost EMI is not available. EMI applies only to courses costing Rs 50,000 or more, and this course costs Rs 30,000.
- The course pause is not available for DSA and Interview Prep.
- Scholarships of up to 30% of the fee are still available based on your entrance test score.
