# Technical Requirements and Class Recordings

## What you need

- A laptop with 8 GB RAM
- A stable internet connection of 10 Mbps
- Chrome or Firefox

## Laptop

You need a laptop with 8 GB RAM. Windows, macOS and Linux all work. A phone or tablet is fine for watching a recording, but it is not enough for the course: you will be writing and running code in every class.

## Internet

You need a stable 10 Mbps connection. Live classes use video and screen sharing, and an unstable connection will cause you to miss parts of the session.

If your connection is weak during a class:

- Turn off your own camera
- Close other tabs and applications that use the network
- Move closer to your router or use a wired connection

## Browser

Live classes and the learner dashboard are supported on Chrome and Firefox. Keep your browser updated to the latest version. Other browsers may work but are not supported, so if you have a problem, switch to Chrome or Firefox before contacting support.

Allow microphone and camera access for the class page when the browser asks.

## Before your first class

1. Sign in to the learner dashboard in Chrome or Firefox.
2. Run the system check on the dashboard home page.
3. Test your microphone and camera.
4. Install the software listed in your course's setup guide.

## Class recordings

- Recordings are available within 24 hours of each live class.
- Recordings are kept for 12 months after the course ends.
- You can find them under the Recordings tab, listed by week.

The Sunday live doubt sessions for DSA and Interview Prep are recorded in the same way.

Recordings are for your personal study. Please do not download and share them.

## Recordings and attendance

Watching a recording does not count as attending a live class. Live-class attendance is tracked separately and matters for placement support eligibility.

## Getting help

If a recording is missing more than 24 hours after a class, or you cannot join a live session, email support@skilvanta.example. Include your course name, your browser and a screenshot of any error. Support is available 9 am to 9 pm IST, every day.
