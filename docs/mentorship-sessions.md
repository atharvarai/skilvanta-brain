# Mentorship Sessions

Every Skilvanta learner is paired with a mentor who works in the industry. This page explains how many sessions you get and the rules for booking, rescheduling and missed sessions.

## What you get

- Two 1:1 mentor sessions per month
- Each session is 45 minutes
- Sessions are held over video call

## What to use a session for

Sessions are yours to direct. Common uses:

- Feedback on an assignment or project
- Help with a topic you are stuck on
- Career questions, such as which roles to target
- Resume and portfolio feedback
- Interview practice

Send your mentor a short agenda before the session so the 45 minutes are used well.

## Booking a session

1. Open the Mentorship tab in your learner dashboard.
2. Pick an open slot from your mentor's calendar.
3. Add a short note on what you want to cover.
4. You will receive a calendar invite with the video link.

## Rescheduling

You can reschedule up to 12 hours before the session. Use the Reschedule button on the booking in your dashboard.

Inside the last 12 hours the session cannot be rescheduled. If you do not attend, it is treated as a no-show.

## No-shows

A no-show counts as a used session. For example, if you miss your first session of the month without rescheduling in time, you have one session left for that month.

Please join on time. If you are having trouble connecting, message your mentor straight away.

## Unused sessions

Unused sessions do not carry over. If you use only one session in a month, you still have two sessions the next month, not three.

## If your mentor cancels

If your mentor cancels or does not join, the session is not counted against you. Rebook from your dashboard, or contact support if you cannot find a suitable slot.

## Changing mentors

If the pairing is not working, email support@skilvanta.example and explain what you are looking for. We will try to match you with a different mentor.
