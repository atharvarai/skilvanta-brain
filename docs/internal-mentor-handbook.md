# Mentor Handbook

This handbook is for Skilvanta mentors. It is an internal document. Do not share it with learners.

## Your role

You are paired with a group of learners and meet each of them 1:1. Each learner is entitled to two sessions per month, 45 minutes each. Your job is to review their work, unblock them, and give honest guidance on their progress and career plans.

You are not expected to re-teach the live classes or to grade assignments.

## Pay

- Mentors are paid Rs 1,200 per completed session.
- A session is complete when it has taken place and you have submitted the session notes.
- Payment is calculated from the session records in the mentor portal, so keep them accurate.

## Session notes

You must submit session notes within 24 hours of each session. Use the notes form in the mentor portal. Include:

- Topics covered
- The learner's progress since the last session
- Action items agreed for the learner
- Any concerns, such as falling behind, low attendance or signs of copied work

Notes are read by the learner-success team and by the placement team, so write them so that someone who was not in the session can follow them.

## Rescheduling and no-shows

- Learners can reschedule up to 12 hours before a session.
- If a learner does not join, wait 15 minutes, then mark the session as a no-show in the portal. A no-show counts as a used session for the learner.
- If you need to cancel, give as much notice as you can and offer alternative slots. A session you cancel is not counted against the learner.

## Escalating a learner

Escalate a learner who misses 3 sessions in a row to the learner-success team. Use the Escalate button on the learner's profile in the mentor portal and add a short summary of your attempts to reach them.

Also escalate, without waiting for three missed sessions, if a learner:

- Tells you they plan to drop out
- Appears to be submitting work that is not their own
- Raises a personal difficulty that is affecting their studies

## What to send to support

Refunds, course pauses, payments and certificates are handled by support. Do not make commitments on these. Ask the learner to email support@skilvanta.example.
