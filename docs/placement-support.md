# Placement Support

Skilvanta helps eligible learners find a job after their course. This page explains who qualifies, what is included and how long it lasts.

## It is not a job guarantee

Placement support is not a job guarantee. We help you prepare and connect you with companies, but hiring decisions are made by employers. We do not promise an offer, a salary or a timeline.

## Eligibility

You must meet all three conditions:

- Complete at least 80% of assignments
- Attend at least 75% of live classes
- Pass 2 mock interviews

You can track your assignment completion and attendance in your learner dashboard. Mock interviews are booked through the Placement tab towards the end of your course.

## What is included

- Resume review: written feedback on your resume, with a second review after you revise it
- Mock interviews: practice interviews with feedback on technical and communication skills
- Referrals to hiring partners: we share your profile with partner companies that have openings matching your skills

## How long it lasts

Placement support lasts 6 months after course completion. After 6 months, support ends whether or not you have accepted an offer.

## How it works

1. Finish your course and meet the eligibility conditions.
2. Submit your resume for review.
3. Pass 2 mock interviews, if you have not already.
4. The placement team shares openings and refers you to hiring partners.
5. You attend interviews and report the outcomes so we can adjust the roles we suggest.

## What we expect from you

- Respond to placement team messages promptly
- Attend every interview you accept
- Tell us when you accept an offer

## Losing placement support

A second plagiarism offence results in removal from placement support. See the page on assignments and academic integrity.

## If you are not yet eligible

If you are short on assignments, you can still submit pending work, subject to the late submission rules. If you are unsure where you stand, email support@skilvanta.example and ask for an eligibility check.
