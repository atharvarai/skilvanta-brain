# Getting Started FAQ

## Which course should I pick?

- New to coding and want to build web applications: Full-Stack Web Development (24 weeks, no prerequisites).
- Know basic Python and want a data role: Data Science and Machine Learning (28 weeks).
- Already know one programming language and want to build backend systems: Backend Engineering with Python (16 weeks).
- Preparing for coding interviews: DSA and Interview Prep (10 weeks, self-paced, no prerequisites).

The course catalog has fees and schedules for all four.

## What do I need before my first class?

A laptop with 8 GB RAM, a stable 10 Mbps internet connection, and Chrome or Firefox. Sign in to your learner dashboard and run the system check before your first class.

## What if I miss a live class?

Class recordings are available within 24 hours. Recordings help you catch up, but they do not count towards live-class attendance, which matters for placement support.

## Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, for courses costing Rs 50,000 or more. No-cost EMI is available over 3, 6 or 9 months. DSA and Interview Prep is not eligible.

## Is there a scholarship?

Yes. Scholarships of up to 30% of the fee are awarded based on your entrance test score. Take the test before you pay.

## Can I get a refund if the course is not for me?

You get a full refund if you request it within 7 days of purchase and have attended fewer than 2 live classes. From day 8 to day 21 the refund is 50%. After day 21 there is no refund.

## How do mentor sessions work?

You get two 1:1 mentor sessions per month, 45 minutes each. Book them from the Mentorship tab in your dashboard.

## Will I get a job?

Skilvanta offers placement support to eligible learners for 6 months after course completion. It is not a job guarantee.

## Will I get a certificate?

Yes, if you finish with an overall score of at least 70%. Certificates are issued within 10 days of course completion.

## How do I get help?

Email support@skilvanta.example. Support is available 9 am to 9 pm IST, every day, with a first response within 24 hours.
