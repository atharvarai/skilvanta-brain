# Refund Policy

This policy applies to all four Skilvanta courses. Days are counted from the date of purchase.

## Refund amounts

| When you request | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Within 7 days of purchase, and you attended fewer than 2 live classes | Full refund |
| Day 8 to day 21 | 50% refund |
| After day 21 | No refund |

## Full refund conditions

Both conditions must be met for a full refund:

- You request the refund within 7 days of purchase.
- You attended fewer than 2 live classes.

If you request within 7 days but have already attended 2 or more live classes, the full refund does not apply.

## Example

A learner buys Full-Stack Web Development for Rs 85,000.

- They request a refund on day 5 after attending one live class: full refund of Rs 85,000.
- They request a refund on day 15: 50% refund, which is Rs 42,500.
- They request a refund on day 25: no refund.

## How to request a refund

Refunds are requested through support.

1. Email support@skilvanta.example from the email address you registered with.
2. Include your full name, the course name and your purchase date.
3. Support confirms your eligibility and the refund amount by email.

The date we receive your email is the date of your request. Support is available 9 am to 9 pm IST, every day, and sends a first response within 24 hours.

## When you will receive the money

Refunds go to the original payment method in 5-7 business days. We cannot send a refund to a different account or card.

If you paid by no-cost EMI, the refund is still sent to the original payment method.

## After a refund

Once a refund is processed, your access to live classes, recordings, mentor sessions and placement support for that course ends.

## Alternatives to a refund

If your reason is a lack of time rather than the course itself, consider a course pause. You can pause once for up to 8 weeks if you request it before week 12. The pause is not available for DSA and Interview Prep.
