# Contacting Support

## How to reach us

- Email: support@skilvanta.example
- Hours: 9 am to 9 pm IST, every day
- First response: within 24 hours

Emails sent outside support hours are picked up when the team is next online. The 24-hour first response applies on weekends and holidays too.

## What to include in your email

Giving us the details upfront saves a round of back-and-forth.

- Your full name and registered email address
- The course you are enrolled in
- A clear description of the problem or request
- Screenshots of any error messages
- For payment issues, the transaction date and amount

Please write from your registered email address. For account and payment requests we can only act on emails from the address on your account.

## What support can help with

- Refund requests
- Course pause requests
- Payment, EMI and scholarship questions
- Problems joining live classes or viewing recordings
- Mentor booking problems and requests to change mentor
- Certificate corrections and delays
- Placement support eligibility checks
- Disputes about late penalties or plagiarism findings

## Where to go for other things

- Questions about course content: ask in the live class, in the Sunday doubt session if you are on DSA and Interview Prep, or in a mentor session.
- Assignment feedback: ask your mentor.
- Job openings and interview scheduling: the placement team will contact you directly once you are eligible.

## Requests with deadlines

Some requests are time-bound. The date we receive your email counts as the date of your request.

- Refunds: a full refund must be requested within 7 days of purchase, and a 50% refund by day 21.
- Course pause: must be requested before week 12.

If you are close to a deadline, send the email first and add details afterwards.

## Following up

Reply on the same email thread rather than starting a new one, so the agent has your full history. If you have not had a first response after 24 hours, reply on the thread and mention that you are following up.

## Feedback

After your request is closed you will get a short survey. We read every response.
