# Certificates

## Who gets a certificate

A certificate is issued to learners who complete their course with an overall score of at least 70%.

This applies to all four courses:

- Full-Stack Web Development
- Data Science and Machine Learning
- Backend Engineering with Python
- DSA and Interview Prep

## When it is issued

Certificates are issued within 10 days of course completion. You will get an email when yours is ready.

## How to check your overall score

Your overall score is shown on the Progress page of your learner dashboard. It is based on your graded work in the course, including assignments and the capstone project where the course has one.

Things that lower your score:

- Late submissions, which lose 10% per day up to 5 days and score zero after that
- Missing assignments, which score zero
- A plagiarism offence, which scores zero on that assignment

Check your score regularly during the course rather than at the end, so you have time to improve it.

## How to download your certificate

1. Sign in to your learner dashboard.
2. Open the Certificates page.
3. Select your course and download the PDF.

Each certificate has your name, the course name, the completion date and a verification link that employers can open to confirm it is genuine.

## Name on the certificate

The certificate uses the name on your Skilvanta profile. Check the spelling before your course ends. If it is wrong on an issued certificate, email support@skilvanta.example with the correct name and we will reissue it.

## If your score is below 70%

Learners with an overall score below 70% do not receive a certificate. You keep access to class recordings for 12 months after the course ends, so you can continue to study the material.

## If your certificate has not arrived

If more than 10 days have passed since you completed the course and your score is at least 70%, email support@skilvanta.example with your registered email address and course name. Support is available 9 am to 9 pm IST, every day.

## Certificates and placement support

The certificate and placement support have separate requirements. Earning a certificate does not automatically make you eligible for placement support. See the placement support page.
