# Placement Statistics 2025

This report is restricted to administrators. Do not share the figures with learners, mentors or support agents, and do not use them in marketing material without approval from the leadership team.

## Headline figures

| Measure | 2025 |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible learners | 412 |
| Learners placed | 318 |
| Placement rate | 77% |
| Median salary | Rs 7.2 LPA |
| Highest salary | Rs 24 LPA |

The placement rate is the number of learners placed divided by the number of eligible learners: 318 of 412, which is 77%.

## Placements by course

| Course | Learners placed |
| --- | --- |
| Full-Stack Web Development | 121 |
| Data Science and Machine Learning | 96 |
| Backend Engineering with Python | 74 |
| DSA and Interview Prep | 27 |
| Total | 318 |

## Definitions

- Eligible learner: a learner who met all three placement support conditions, which are at least 80% of assignments completed, at least 75% live-class attendance, and passing 2 mock interviews.
- Placed: an eligible learner who accepted a job offer within the 6-month placement support period after course completion.
- Salary: the annual figure stated in the offer letter, in lakhs per annum (LPA).
- Median and highest salary are calculated across all 318 placed learners.

Learners who did not meet the eligibility conditions are not counted in either the eligible or the placed figure.

## Notes on reading the figures

- 94 eligible learners were not placed within their support period.
- Full-Stack Web Development accounts for the largest number of placements, followed by Data Science and Machine Learning.
- DSA and Interview Prep has the fewest placements. Many of its learners are already employed or take it alongside another course.
- Per-course placement counts are not placement rates. Per-course eligible counts are not included in this report.

## Data sources

- Eligibility: learner dashboard records for assignments, attendance and mock interviews
- Placement and salary: offer letters submitted to the placement team

## Using these figures externally

Placement support is described publicly as support, not a job guarantee. Any external use of these numbers must keep that wording and must be approved first.
