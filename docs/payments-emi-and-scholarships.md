# Payments, EMI and Scholarships

## Course fees

| Course | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Full-Stack Web Development | Rs 85,000 |
| Data Science and Machine Learning | Rs 95,000 |
| Backend Engineering with Python | Rs 60,000 |
| DSA and Interview Prep | Rs 30,000 |

You can pay the full fee upfront or, for eligible courses, by no-cost EMI.

## No-cost EMI

No-cost EMI is available over 3, 6 or 9 months for courses costing Rs 50,000 or more.

- Eligible: Full-Stack Web Development, Data Science and Machine Learning, Backend Engineering with Python
- Not eligible: DSA and Interview Prep, because its fee is Rs 30,000

"No-cost" means the total you pay across all instalments equals the course fee. For example, Backend Engineering with Python at Rs 60,000 over 6 months is Rs 10,000 per month.

To use EMI:

1. Choose your course and select EMI at checkout.
2. Pick a tenure of 3, 6 or 9 months.
3. Complete the verification steps shown by the payment provider.

## Scholarships

Scholarships of up to 30% of the fee are awarded based on your entrance test score. A higher score earns a larger scholarship, up to the 30% limit.

- The entrance test is taken online before you pay.
- Your scholarship percentage is shown with your test result.
- The scholarship is applied as a reduction in the fee at checkout.
- Scholarships are available on all four courses.

For example, the maximum scholarship on Data Science and Machine Learning (Rs 95,000) is Rs 28,500.

## Using EMI with a scholarship

Whether a course qualifies for EMI depends on what the course costs. If you have a scholarship and want to pay by EMI, ask support to confirm your options before you check out.

## Refunds

Refunds go to the original payment method in 5-7 business days. See the refund policy for who qualifies and how much is refunded.

## Payment problems

If a payment fails or you were charged twice, email support@skilvanta.example with your registered email address and a screenshot of the transaction. Support is available 9 am to 9 pm IST, every day.
