# Course Pause Policy

If something comes up and you cannot keep up with your course for a while, you can pause it instead of dropping out.

## The rules

- You get one pause per course.
- A pause can be up to 8 weeks long.
- You must request the pause before week 12 of your course.
- The pause is not available for DSA and Interview Prep.

## Which courses allow a pause

| Course | Pause available |
| --- | --- |
| Full-Stack Web Development | Yes |
| Data Science and Machine Learning | Yes |
| Backend Engineering with Python | Yes |
| DSA and Interview Prep | No |

DSA and Interview Prep is self-paced over 10 weeks, so it does not have a pause option.

## How to request a pause

1. Email support@skilvanta.example before week 12 of your course.
2. Include your course name, the date you want the pause to start and how many weeks you need (up to 8).
3. Support confirms the pause and your return date by email.

Support is available 9 am to 9 pm IST, every day, and responds within 24 hours. Send your request early; a request made in week 12 or later cannot be approved.

## What happens during a pause

- You do not attend live classes and no assignments fall due.
- You keep access to recordings of the classes you have already covered.
- Mentor sessions are not available while you are paused.

## Coming back

When your pause ends, you rejoin the course at the point where you stopped, in a batch that is covering that week. Your new batch follows the same weekly class schedule for the course. Your earlier assignment scores and attendance are kept.

You can return earlier than planned by writing to support.

## Things to keep in mind

- The pause can be used only once per course. If you return after 3 weeks, the remaining weeks cannot be used later.
- A pause does not change the refund timeline, which is counted from your purchase date.
- If you do not return when your pause ends, contact support to discuss your options.
